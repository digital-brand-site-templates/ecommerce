About Us

  • Home

The New Way to advance.

Flowers-Petal started in 2014 as a Saturday stall and became a shop when the queue got longer than the stall. Two florists, one van and a walled garden on the edge of town that grows about a third of what we sell — the rest comes from the flower market at five in the morning, three days a week. We do the everyday (a bunch for the kitchen table), the once-a-year (birthdays, anniversaries) and the once-in-a-lifetime (we've done about two hundred weddings). What we won't do is send you something that looks like the photo but was cut a week ago: if it isn't fresh enough to last five days, it doesn't leave the shop.
