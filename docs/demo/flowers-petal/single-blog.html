Three hundred stems, no water, one barn

  • 22 july 2017
  • by Admin
  • in Weddings
  • 2 Comments
  • 40 views

A wedding in a barn with no water and no power, a June heatwave, and three hundred stems that had to look alive at nine in the evening. Peonies and roses were out; dahlias, zinnias and eucalyptus were in. We cut on the Friday evening, conditioned everything overnight in the cold room, and drove up at six. The arch went up in shade cloth and came out of it an hour before the ceremony. Nobody noticed, which is the point.

“Nobody at the wedding knew the arch had been under shade cloth an hour earlier — which is exactly what you want from wedding flowers in a heatwave.”

The lesson we took home: pick the flowers for the room, not the mood board. Dahlias, zinnias and eucalyptus shrug off a hot afternoon; peonies and garden roses don't. We say this in every June consultation now, and we've never had a bride regret it.

If you're planning a summer wedding somewhere without water or power, tell us early. We'll bring the buckets, the shade cloth and the delivery window nobody else will agree to — and the flowers will still look alive at nine in the evening.
