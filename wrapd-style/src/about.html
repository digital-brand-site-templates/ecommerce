About Wrapd-Style

An independent clothing and beauty store on Mill Street since 2012 — and online since 2017.

We started with a rail of dresses and a table of lipsticks in a room above a bakery. The idea was simple: sell only what we'd wear ourselves, at prices that don't need a sale to make sense. We still work that way. Every piece is worn, washed and tried before it goes on the site; if it doesn't hold up, it doesn't go in.

Orders are packed by the same people who chose the stock, shipped within two days, and free to return for thirty. Sizes run true — we measure everything ourselves and print the measurements on each product page.

What we stand for

Made to last

Natural fibres, proper seams, buttons that stay on. Fewer, better things.

Honest prices

One price all year. No inflated "was" tags, no permanent sale.

Easy returns

Thirty days, free, no questions. Try it at home, decide at home.
